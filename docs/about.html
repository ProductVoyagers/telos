What is Telos

The PM toolkit where every screen is judged against the business goal.

Telos turns a wireframe into a styled screen, then critiques it against the result you're trying to move — not against generic design taste.

The problem

Nobody checks, before shipping, whether a screen actually serves the goal.

Design tools stop at
"Does it look good?"
Analytics tools stop at
"What did the numbers do?" — weeks after launch.

Between the two is a gap: the moment before you build, when you could still ask whether this screen will move the metric you care about. That question usually gets skipped — so teams ship pretty screens that don't change the number.

The one question Telos asks

Does this screen serve the KR we're trying to move?
Increase 7-day retention Lift checkout conversion Grow weekly active use

A KR (Key Result) is the measurable goal. It's what separates a Telos review from a UX audit: every finding is tied to the business outcome, not to a style rule. No KR, no review — Telos asks for it first.

What it does

01
Learn
Learns your design system from a few screenshots
02
Create
Turns a wireframe into a styled screen in your look
03
Critique
Scores the screen 1–5 against your KR
04
Judge
A second agent grades the critique's quality
05
Improve
You pick fixes; it regenerates and re-scores

Why it's different

Anchored to the goal, not taste. Every finding connects to the KR. It's an impact review, not a UX opinion.
A judge, not just a generator. One agent writes the critique; a separate, read-only agent scores how trustworthy it is — so you get a confidence number before anything reaches leadership.
You stay in control. You choose which recommendations to apply, every version is kept, and you can revert at any time.
Local-first and open-source. Nothing leaves your machine until you connect a repo. Apache-2.0 — it's yours.

Who it's for

Product managers and founders who want to pressure-test a screen against a goal before it ships — and squads who want a shared, goal-annotated view of every screen they're building.
